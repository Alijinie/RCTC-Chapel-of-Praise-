It is my joy to welcome you into a house where sound doctrine and the Spirit's fire meet. RCTC Chapel of Praise exists to raise a generation of ambassadors who will carry the gospel with both academic excellence and supernatural power.

Whether you are here as a student minister, a faculty member, or a first-time visitor, know that you have been received into a family that will disciple, correct, and celebrate you. May you find here a covering that will nurture you for the assignment ahead.

THE CHAPLAIN

Pastor. Daniel

Beloved of God,

The altar of this chapel stays lit because of you — your worship, your obedience, and your prayers. As Chaplain, my heart is to see every member of this campus family walking in wholeness: spirit, soul, and body.

Come as you are. Bring your burdens to the Prayer Desk, your questions to Digging Deep, and your praise to the Sunday altar, and let us carry it all to heaven together. Heaven is listening, and God still answers by fire.

Yours in the Master's service,
The Chaplain
