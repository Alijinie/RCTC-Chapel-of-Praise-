GOOD TO KNOW

Events FAQ

Do I need to register for Sunday services? +

No — just come. Registration is only for first-time guests who want protocol to reserve seats, and for outreaches needing logistics.

How do campus shuttles work for night programs? +

Buses leave the Chapel forecourt 2 hours before Arena programs (e.g. 5:00 PM for Holy Ghost Service) and return after dismissal with security escort.

Can teens and children attend Faith Clinic? +

Yes, with a parent or mentor. Teens sit in the front-side section with mentors on Thursdays.

How do I volunteer at an event? +

Tap any “Remind me” to save the date, then use “Join the outreach team” to enrol in protocol, media, prayer or welfare units.

      RCCG Global Mandate · 2032

      Vision 2032: The Global Mandate

      Explosive Growth · Deep Discipleship · 40 Million Souls

      Anchored on the prophetic mandate given to the General Overseer, Pastor E.A. Adeboye, Vision 2032 is the current, defining strategic roadmap for the Redeemed Christian Church of God worldwide — a global charge to accelerate evangelism, multiply fellowships, and expand the Kingdom of God exponentially.

      1. The Core Objective — "The 40-400 Mandate"

      The vision moves the body of Christ from linear addition to explosive multiplication, focusing on two distinct targets:

      40 Million Members — expanding global active church membership and regular attendance.
      400,000 Parishes & units — averaging 100 members each, for close-knit discipleship and personal care.

      2. Our Strategic Pillars

      To bring this global harvest to reality, the mission relies on three operational pillars across all regions:

      A. Operation 1-1-1 — Every Member a Soul Winner

      Evangelism is decentralized from being a church department to becoming a personal lifestyle for every believer. Every member is challenged, equipped, and mobilized to win and disciple one soul, every month — through intentional personal evangelism ("Each One Reach One") within their immediate circles of influence.

      B. Boundaryless Church Planting

      The vision breaks away from traditional brick-and-mortar limitations to meet people exactly where they are — maintaining a place of worship within a 5-minute walk in developing nations and a 5-minute drive in developed nations, through street fellowships, school campuses, office boardrooms, parks, and Mobile Churches.

      C. Youth Mobilization & Digital Discipleship

      Identifying, training, and releasing ministry-inclined youth to pioneer new units, cells, and expressions of church — and using digital platforms not just for broadcast, but as active tools for online soul winning, virtual small groups, and remote discipleship.

      3. The Prophetic Milestone

      Vision 2032 represents a covenant milestone for the mission. As our General Overseer, Pastor E.A. Adeboye, approaches his 90th birthday, this collective effort serves as a spiritual birthday gift and a profound testimony to God's faithfulness — proving that the small seed planted decades ago continues to cover the entire earth.

      4. Our Local Action at RCTC Chapel of Praise

      As a local assembly, RCTC Chapel of Praise is fully aligned with this global engine. We are transitioning from spectators to active harvesters by:

      • Going Out — driving consistent, localized neighborhood evangelism and community outreaches.
      • Planting Inward — nurturing, supporting, and multiplying our House Fellowship centers.
      • Let's go a Fishing — Parish planting, systematically discipling, and fully integrating every new convert into the Church of God.
      "The harvest truly is plentiful, but the laborers are few. We rise, we build, and we move!"

      RCCG Global Evangelism · Matt 4:19

      Let's Go A-Fishing

      "Fishers of men" · Christmas & Easter editions

      "Let's Go A-Fishing" is the flagship global evangelism and soul-winning initiative of The Redeemed Christian Church of God, initiated under the visionary leadership of the General Overseer, Pastor E.A. Adeboye. Held bi-annually during the Christmas and Easter seasons, it unites parishes worldwide in one mission: to step outside the church walls, spread the Gospel, plant new churches, and transform lives through community impact.

      Core Pillars of the Program

      Mass Evangelism & Revivals

      Street-level witnessing, open-air crusades, and mega-rallies targeting towns, villages, and cities worldwide.

      Church Planting

      Rapidly establishing new parishes in unreached territories to nurture new converts.

      Christian Social Responsibility (CSR)

      Demonstrating the love of Christ tangibly — free community healthcare (medical checkups, prescription drugs, eye surgeries) and distributing food, clothing, and welfare items to the less privileged.

      Event Calendar & Daily Structure

      Christmas Edition Dec 23 – Dec 26
      Easter Edition Good Friday – Easter Monday
      • Morning — Morning Cry / neighbourhood street evangelism.
      • Afternoon — Community welfare outreaches & free medical clinics.
      • Evening — Crossroads crusades, film outreaches, and revival services.
